# EverMap Forever

EverMap Forever lets you restyle and reorganize your minimap, and it’s built for WoW Forever. You can change how the map looks, move its parts around, and hide what you don’t need.

Features :
--------------------
- **Map shapes:** use a circle, square, diamond, hexagon, heart, octagon and more, instead of the default round map.
- **Borders and backdrop:** pick from border presets, make your own, or save your current look as a preset.
- **Buttons:** show each minimap button always, only on hover, or never, and drag them wherever you like. This includes the Day/Night indicator.
- **Zone text, clock and coordinates:** place them where you want and style them with your own font and colors.
- **Movable minimap:** drag it, lock it in place, and set its scale.
- **Auto zoom-out:** after you zoom in, the map zooms back out on its own after a delay you choose.
- **HUD map and pings:** a HUD map you can toggle with a keybinding, and ping notices that show who pinged.
- **Other movable elements:** move the top-of-screen objective tracker and the capture bar.

Usage :
-----------------------
Right-click the minimap, or type 	<sub>/evermap</sub> or /minimap</sub>, to open the options.

Installation :
------------------------------
Put the 	<sub>EverMapForever</sub> folder in 	<sub>Interface\AddOns</sub> and restart the game.
